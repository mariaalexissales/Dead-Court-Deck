title=Dead Court Deck
description=[h1]Dead Court Deck[/h1]
description=[h2]Rip packs, chase the court, cash in the suit[/h2]
description=[b]A collectible card set for Build 42. Find packs out in Knox Country, open them, and trade a completed suit in for loot you decide on.[/b]
description=
description=Every card comes in four finishes, and the court cards come in four races - so the deck you are actually chasing is [b]352 cards[/b] deep.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]
description=[b]1. Find packs.[/b] They turn up where a deck of cards would - bedroom dressers and nightstands, desks, rec room shelves, school lockers, and the toy and hobby shelves in shops. Now and then a zombie is carrying one.
description=
description=[b]2. Open them.[/b] Right-click a pack and hit [b]Open Card Pack[/b]. Five cards, each rolled on its own, duplicates and all. Sitting on a stack? [b]Open All Card Packs[/b] does the lot in one go and calls out the good pulls.
description=
description=[b]3. Check the finish.[/b] Every single card rolls one:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Standard[/b] - the common print, coloured by suit. Red hearts, cyan spades, black clubs, green diamonds.
description=[*] [b]Bronze[/b], [b]Silver[/b] and [b]Gold[/b] - the metal prints, in that order of rarity. Gold is genuinely rare. You will know when one lands.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]4. Mind the court.[/b] Jack, Queen and King are the [b]court cards[/b], and every one of them belongs to a race - [b]Human[/b], [b]Goblin[/b], [b]Elf[/b] or [b]Dwarf[/b]. They are the rare pull, and the race is what decides your payout. Aces and numbers have no race.
description=
description=[b]5. Build a suit.[/b] A full suit is [b]13 cards[/b]: Ace through 10, plus a Jack, a Queen and a King. All 13 in the same finish, and all three court cards of the same race. So [i]Gold Goblin Hearts[/i] is a real set - a Gold Jack of Hearts sitting next to a Silver Queen is not.
description=
description=[b]6. Cash it in.[/b] Right-click any card in that set and pick [b]Trade In Full Suit[/b]. It takes the 13 cards and pays you out.
description=
description=[b]Not there yet?[/b] The option greys out instead of disappearing, and its tooltip names the set you are closest to and lists exactly which cards you are still missing. That is your checklist - no extra window to go hunting for.
description=
description=[i]Cards sitting in your bags count too, so you do not have to carry the collection in your hands.[/i]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Setting Up Rewards[/h2]
description=[b]What a suit pays out is yours to decide, in one .json file. No Lua, no repacking, no restart.[/b]
description=
description=The first launch writes the file for you, at [b]Zomboid\Lua\DeadCourtDeck\rewards.json[/b] - on a server, that is the Zomboid folder of whichever machine runs it. Open it up and you get this:
description=
description=[code]{
description=  "rarityMultiplier": {
description=    "Standard": 1,
description=    "Bronze": 2,
description=    "Silver": 3,
description=    "Gold": 5
description=  },
description=  "races": {
description=    "Human":  { "items": [ { "item": "Base.MetalPipe", "count": 2 } ] },
description=    "Goblin": { "items": [ { "item": "Base.Money", "count": 5 } ] },
description=    "Elf":    { "items": [ { "item": "Base.BookForaging2", "count": 1 } ] },
description=    "Dwarf":  { "items": [ { "item": "Base.HandAxeForged", "count": 1 } ] }
description=  }
description=}[/code]
description=
description=[b]The payout is keyed to the race of the court cards in the suit[/b], and each count is multiplied by the finish. With the table above, a Standard Human suit hands over 2 pipes - the Gold Human suit hands over 10.
description=
description=[b]What ships with the mod is placeholder junk.[/b] Pipes, cash, a foraging book and a forged axe, there purely so the system does something out of the box. Swap in whatever fits your server, and put as many entries in a race's list as you like - items from your other mods included.
description=
description=Edits apply on the [b]next trade-in[/b]. No restart, no reload button, nobody kicked. And if you fumble the JSON, the last good table stays loaded and the console tells you what upset it, instead of everyone's payout quietly becoming nothing.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Sandbox Options[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Court Card Chance[/b] - how often a card comes out a Jack, Queen or King instead of an Ace or a number
description=[*] [b]Standard / Bronze / Silver / Gold Weight[/b] - the odds of each finish, weighed against each other
description=[*] [b]Human / Goblin / Elf / Dwarf Court Weight[/b] - which races turn up, and how often
description=[*] [b]Pack Loot Multiplier[/b] - how thick packs are on the ground. 0 turns world spawns off entirely
description=[*] [b]Zombie Pack Drop Chance[/b] - how often the dead are carrying one. 0 turns it off
description=[/list]
description=Out of the box a first full suit lands somewhere around 60 packs, and the metal sets take a good deal longer. Crank the court chance if that is too long a grind for your server.
description=
